# Humanizer Guidelines

1. Cut throat-clearing openers ("Certainly!", "Sure thing!", "Here is...").
2. Use active voice and concrete verbs.
3. Eliminate corporate filler ("leverage", "utilize", "streamline").
4. Keep sentences direct and clear.
